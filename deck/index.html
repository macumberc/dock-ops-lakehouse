The Problem

Docks are the bottleneck — and no one can see them

At Meridian's cross-dock terminals, trailers arrive in unpredictable waves. Doors sit idle at 6am, then get slammed by noon. Supervisors staff by gut feel from a fixed shift plan that never changes.

What it costs (measured over 31 days, 3 terminals)

  • $235,799 in trailer detention fees — carriers billing us for waiting past the free window
  • $108,780 in avoidable overtime — reactive PM catch-up crews
  • Waits up to 13.5 hours for a door during the mid-day crunch

Why it persists

  • No real-time visibility into which doors are occupied
  • No forecast of when the wave hits — staffing is a flat guess
  • Camera feeds exist but no one watches 36 of them at once
Root Cause, In the Data

A fixed shift plan that misses the mid-day loading crunch
